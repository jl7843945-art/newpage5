Titanium Tube & Pipe

Titanium tube and pipe to ASTM B338, B861 and B862 — seamless and welded tube, in Gr.1, Gr.2 and Gr.7, cut to length for heat exchangers, condensers and process piping.

Supply forms

  • Seamless tube and pipe — ASTM B861, for pressure and critical heat-transfer service.
  • Welded tube and pipe — ASTM B862, cost-efficient for condensers and process lines.
  • Heat-exchanger tube — ASTM B338, the standard reference for exchanger and condenser tubing.
  • Cut-to-length — tubes cut to exchanger or spool dimensions.

Typical applications

Titanium tube is the standard answer to seawater and chloride corrosion: heat exchangers and condensers in power and desalination plants, offshore cooling and firewater systems, chemical process piping, and heat-transfer bundles where stainless steel would pit. Gr.1 is often chosen for maximum formability and corrosion resistance; Gr.2 where higher strength is needed.

Inspection and documents

Dimensional checks, eddy-current or hydrostatic testing and surface inspection are performed to the agreed standard, with mill certificates to EN 10204 3.1 and heat-number traceability on every bundle. Third-party inspection can be arranged when specified in the order.

Seamless or welded titanium tube — which do I need?

Seamless tube (ASTM B861) is normally specified for higher pressure, tighter tolerances and critical heat-exchanger service. Welded tube (ASTM B862) is widely used where wall thickness and cost efficiency matter more, for example condenser and process piping. We confirm the right option against your design pressure and medium.

Can you supply tubes cut to length for heat exchangers?

Yes. Tubes are supplied cut to the length required by your exchanger design, with end condition, tolerance and inspection scope agreed in the order.
