About ALLOYTI

A titanium manufacturer working across aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications — TA1, TC4 (Gr.5) and TA15, to ASTM, GB and AMS standards.

Across aerospace, marine engineering, medical and new-energy applications, titanium alloys combine strength, corrosion resistance, light weight and biocompatibility in a way no other structural metal does. That combination is the reason titanium keeps replacing stainless steel and nickel alloys in service where chloride, temperature or weight decide the outcome.

What ALLOYTI does

ALLOYTI focuses on the R&D, production and deep processing of titanium and titanium alloys. We cover mainstream grades — TA1, TC4 (Gr.5 / Ti-6Al-4V) and TA15 — and deliver one-stop solutions in plate, bar, tube, fasteners and precision forgings to ASTM, GB and AMS standards. Our products are exported to North America, Europe, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.

Production and processing

Our site covers the steps that decide whether a titanium order arrives usable: coil slitting and cut-to-width processing, straightening of bar and tube, heat treatment to the required delivery condition, turning and machining of bar and forgings, and dimensional inspection before packing. Titanium discs and rings are held in stock so that repeat sizes can be shipped without waiting for a production slot. See the production site.

Certifications

  • ISO 9001 — quality management system.
  • AS9100D — aerospace quality management system.

Certificate copies and numbers are available on request. Any further approvals referenced in an enquiry are confirmed against current certificates before we commit to them.

How we work with buyers

We supply more than material. Before an order is placed we confirm grade, standard, dimensions, tolerances, inspection scope and documents item by item — so the quotation you receive already reflects what can actually be produced and certified. After the order, we track packing, marking, inspection and dispatch milestones and keep you updated at the points that matter.

  • Grade selection advice when the service conditions are still open.
  • Process support — machining allowances, heat-treatment condition, surface finish.
  • Document support — EN 10204 certificates, packing lists and export paperwork.
  • Fast response — enquiries answered within one business day.

For quality procedures and the full document list, see Quality & Service. For grade and lead-time questions, see the sourcing FAQ.
