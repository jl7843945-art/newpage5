Titanium Forgings & Machined Parts

Precision titanium forgings and machined components — discs, rings, blocks and stepped shafts in TC4 (Gr.5), TA15 and commercially pure grades, finished to drawing.

Forging and machining capability

  • Discs and rings — rolled or hammer-forged, held in stock in common sizes.
  • Blocks and slabs — for machining into structural or pressure-containing parts.
  • Stepped shafts and flanges — forged to drawing to reduce machining waste.
  • CNC machining — turning, milling and drilling of forgings and bar stock to your drawing.
  • Heat treatment — in-house heat treatment lines to achieve the required delivery condition.

Typical applications

Forged titanium is used where grain flow, integrity and strength-to-weight matter: aerospace structural fittings and engine-adjacent parts, offshore and subsea components, high-performance machinery, and pressure-containing parts in chemical service. Machined titanium parts to drawing are produced for customers who prefer to receive finished components rather than stock.

How to specify a forging order

Send the drawing (PDF, DWG or DXF), grade, quantity, heat-treatment condition and inspection requirements. We confirm forging route, machining allowance, tolerances and documents before quotation. Ultrasonic testing, penetrant inspection and EN 10204 3.1 or 3.2 certificates are arranged as agreed — see Quality & Service.

Related products

Titanium bar for machining · titanium plate · titanium fasteners.

What forging shapes can you produce?

Discs, rings, blocks, stepped shafts, flanges and stepped or contoured blanks — produced to your drawing in TC4 (Gr.5), TA15 or commercially pure grades, then machined, heat treated and inspected as agreed.

Do you machine forgings to finished drawings?

Yes. Forgings can be delivered as blanks or fully machined. Our site includes turning and dimensional inspection; final tolerances, surface finish and inspection scope are confirmed in the order.
